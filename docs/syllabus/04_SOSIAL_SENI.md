# Sosial & Seni / Social Studies & Arts — Syllabus

**Status:** DRAFT v2 2026-10-16 (enriched: essentials gaps closed, hidden gems per level). Casual mode. Two strands in one subject: **Sosial** (geography, history, civic life, economy & money, culture, media) and **Seni** (visual art, music, dance & theatre, craft & design, film & story, food as culture). Each level mixes both; the challenge step usually combines them. Gems follow the framework §1b and carry a `source` note.

Sensitivity rules: history and civic units stick to widely accepted facts and present contested matters as "ada beberapa pandangan / there are several views"; no party politics; religions and ethnic groups described respectfully and, where possible, by people from those communities; a social-studies reviewer and a cultural reviewer sign each pack. Art units are *doing* units: many steps end with a "Buat sendiri / Make it yourself" info_card done offline; the app never grades artwork, it gives a sticker for showing up. Child-safety content (kids Pemula unit 9) is written with a child-protection reviewer.

Essentials spine: **self & community → map & nation → time & history → money & economy → rules & civic life → culture & diversity → media & information → making & appreciating art**, deeper each level.
