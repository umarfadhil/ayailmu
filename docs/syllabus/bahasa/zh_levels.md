## 0. How these levels work

- **Same conventions as Pemula** (`zh_pemula.md` §0): Simplified characters, Mainland Putonghua; pinyin with tone
  marks in citation tones, sandhi never written (一直 yīzhí, 不过 bùguò, 一张 yī zhāng, 可以 kěyǐ); neutral-tone
  syllables unmarked (了 le, 吗 ma, 喜欢 xǐhuan, 便宜 piányi, 左边 zuǒbian); sentences capitalised; 儿 as -r (哪儿 nǎr,
  这儿 zhèr, 有点儿 yǒudiǎnr); "kamu" for kids, "Anda" for adults.
- **Who sees pinyin.** Kids: on every Chinese card, always. Adults: a card shows pinyin only in an item where at least
  one Chinese card is **new** — not on a card in the adults Pemula pack, in a lower adult level, or in an earlier unit of
  the same pack. The decision is per item, so a lone pinyin card never marks the answer. Text lines (model answers,
  screens, help lines) always give pinyin. **Reading items** (adults only, `P-READ` «Baca, lalu pilih gambarnya.»):
  a known word with no pinyin and no speaker, picture options — the first pinyin-free reading test (Pemula open
  question 1): `ZHO-A-M-02-006`, `ZHO-A-H-01-010`, `ZHO-A-X-02-010`.
- **Picture first** (contract §1, §8): every concrete word has its picture; the new §6 keys are used for routine,
  clothes, town & travel, weather & seasons, hobbies, technology and `val_angry_face`; clock faces (`clock_*`) carry
  the time words and the arrow glyphs carry the directions. Text-only options remain for numbers, prices, times,
  sentence meanings and the agree/disagree baskets. Shares per pack: `zho-k-m` 23/24 = 96% · `zho-a-m` 22/24 = 92% · `zho-k-h` 22/24 = 92% · `zho-a-h` 21/24 = 88% · `zho-k-x` 21/24 = 88% · `zho-a-x` 20/24 = 83%.
- **Sound.** Every Chinese card carries its clip except in an `audio_match` (a speaker would play the answer) and on
  reading items. Every pack has 6 `audio_match` items, hidden until the ElevenLabs clips exist; no REMED item needs a
  clip; every skill has a scored PRACT item without audio; every unit keeps ≥ 5 scored items without audio.
- **Trace.** Only the 25 Pemula characters have stroke data; each pack reviews one of them inside a new word (上 in
  上学, 中 in 中午, 下 in 下雨). No new trace glyphs.
- **Gestures:** `match_pairs` `drag` (picture → word) or `connect` (word → meaning/number, arrows → words);
  `sort_buckets` for measure words, 坐/骑, 热/冷, yes/no and agree/disagree; `order` for the day, the seasons and the
  dialogues (`P-DIALOG` «Urutkan percakapannya.»); `tiles` and `fill_blank` with `drag` for sentences and particles.
- **Grammar, lightly:** time before the action; 两 before measure words; 很 before adjectives; 了 after a verb
  (completed: 买了) and at the end of a sentence (new situation: 下雨了) — adults Mahir only; 会 for the future;
  因为…所以… (kids Master); 请 + verb, 可以…吗？, 我觉得…, 有点儿, 不过 (adults Master).

## 8. Mistakes Indonesian speakers make (new at these levels)

1. **Time before the action.** Indonesian puts the time last (*bangun jam tujuh*); Mandarin puts it first
   (七点起床). Tiles items in every Menengah unit 01 and the REMED tiles enforce the order.
2. **二 vs 两.** *Dua* is one word; Mandarin uses 两 before measure words and for 2 o'clock (两点). Tiles with both
   二 and 两 as distractors (Menengah 02, Mahir 02 adults), the adults Mahir puzzle (下午两点).
3. **Measure words.** Indonesian *se-* + noun helps (*secangkir*), but 件/条/双/张 have no equivalents. Baskets and
   fill-the-gap items; 本 for books, 张 for flat things (tickets, maps, beds, tables).
4. **Vehicle before 去.** *Ke taman naik sepeda* → 骑自行车去公园 (kids Mahir tiles; reviewed from Pemula).
5. **了 is not a tense.** Learners map 了 onto *sudah* everywhere. Adults Mahir shows the two uses separately
   (买了两张票 done; 下雨了 new situation) and contrasts 会 (future) in a tiles item; kids meet only 下雨了！ / 下雪了！
   as a fixed exclamation.
6. **很 before adjectives.** *Aku capek* → 我很累 (not 我累 in neutral statements); 很 appears in every feelings and
   weather tiles item.
7. **The right verb for a hobby.** *Main* covers football, badminton and guitar; Mandarin needs 踢, 打, 弹 (kids Master
   baskets and fill items; the daily puzzle).
8. **因为…所以… both present.** Indonesian drops *jadi* after *karena*; Mandarin usually keeps both (kids Master tiles
   and both REMED fill items).
9. **您 and 请.** *Anda* ↔ 您 is natural for Indonesians; the trap is 请 placed after the verb. Fill item 老师，您好,
   tiles 请问，您贵姓.
10. **有点儿 vs 一点儿.** 有点儿 + adjective is a complaint (有点儿贵); 一点儿 after an adjective is a request
    (便宜一点). Both appear in the shop dialogue and the opinion tiles; the screen says which is which.
11. **Tones in new minimal pairs:** 买 mǎi / 卖 mài (Pemula), 热 rè vs 累 lèi vowels, 睡觉 shuìjiào vs 水饺 shuǐjiǎo,
    种花 zhòng huā (tone 4, not zhǒng). Reviewers should listen for these in the clips.
