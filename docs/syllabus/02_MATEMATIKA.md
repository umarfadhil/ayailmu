# Matematika / Mathematics — Syllabus

**Status:** DRAFT v2 2026-10-16 (enriched: essentials gaps closed, hidden gems per level). Casual mode (`00_FRAMEWORK.md` §3). Kids levels align loosely with Kurikulum Merdeka SD phases (A: kelas 1–2, B: 3–4, C: 5–6); adults follow numeracy → money and decisions → quantitative thinking → the beauty of it. All contexts Indonesian (Rp, kg, km, °C) in both languages.

Design notes: every unit opens with a picture problem, not a formula; `estimate_slider` before computing trains number sense; speed rounds are opt-in and never below Mahir for kids (CR-ENG-9); mistakes get a drawn "look again" hint, not a red X; stickers are hand-drawn objects from the unit. Gems follow the mechanic in the framework §1b and carry a `source` note.

Essentials spine (what every learner leaves each level with): **number sense → operations → fractions/ratio → measurement & geometry → data & chance → algebraic thinking → reasoning and proof**, plus money and time at every level.
