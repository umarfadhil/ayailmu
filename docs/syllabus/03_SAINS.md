# Sains / Science — Syllabus

**Status:** DRAFT v2 2026-10-16 (enriched: essentials gaps closed, hidden gems per level). Casual mode. Kids units draw on IPAS (SD) themes; adults get "how the world actually works" with Indonesian examples. Every unit includes a **"Coba di rumah / Try at home"** info_card (safe, cheap, no app feature) and a **"Mitos atau fakta / Myth or fact"** true_false round. Gems follow the framework §1b and carry a `source` note.

Safety and accuracy: a science reviewer signs every pack; health units state facts and point to professionals, never diagnose; first-aid content is awareness-level and says "call 119 / go to the puskesmas"; no kids' experiment uses fire, chemicals beyond kitchen items, or electricity above battery level. Units touching the changing body (kids Mahir) are flagged for the reviewer and written for ages 10–12.

Essentials spine: **living things → matter & energy → Earth & space → the human body & health → scientific method → technology & society**, present at every level with increasing depth.
