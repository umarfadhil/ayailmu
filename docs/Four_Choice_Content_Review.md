## What changed from v0.2

- **New IDs, same lineages.** v0.3 items use `A3-*`/`B3-*` IDs. Each keeps the lineage of the material it continues, and lists the v0.1/v0.2 IDs it replaces (`legacy_item_ids`). Old answers are never re-scored or relabelled.
- **Vocabulary is taught before it is tested.** New lesson screens DEMO-S4 (six Unit A words) and DEMO-BS4 (seven Unit B verbs) come before any scored item that needs them. Every scored item lists the lemmas it relies on (`requires_taught`), and a test checks that each one appears on a lesson screen of its unit.
- **No answer-style shortcut.** Unglossed Unit A sentences now use a 2×2 grid: gender × predicate meaning, with the meanings drawn from the taught table. Glossed sentences (meaning supplied) use gender × description-versus-possession/speech. The guided possession/speech focus questions hold gender constant. Form grids show both forms in the help line (`نَادِمٌ / نَادِمَةٌ`), so the masculine key is not copied from it.
- **Wording.** Possession options now read «X milik dia (…)» instead of «…-nya (sesuatu yang ia miliki)». Slash keys are gone (W-A2 «orang yang saleh», W-A5 «orang yang berilmu»). كَرِيمٌ stays «mulia» and is marked **[CHECK]** for Arabic review; no choice offers «dermawan», so the key is not ambiguous within its options.
- **Unit B REMED added.** B3-R1 شَكَرَتْ and B3-R2 سَجَدَتْ are unseen feminine forms of the source REMED verbs. Their masculine meanings are taught (unscored) on DEMO-BS4. An earlier interim draft used خرج/ذهب forms already shown elsewhere; it was rejected, and a regression test now proves the freshness check rejects those literal interim items.
- **Reservations retired.** Teaching رَجَعَ and سَمِعَ on DEMO-BS4 means they can no longer be fresh holdouts, so the v0.2 READY item B2-Y1 and D7 item B2-D7V1 are retired (not in v0.3). The v0.2 word card B2-W4 (رَجَعَتْ) is replaced by B3-W4 (masculine رَجَعَ). Retired IDs keep their lineage in the history registry. **Unit B now has no READY or D7 item; its only timed check is D30 (B3-D30V1, سَمِعَتْ).** That coverage is reported as partial/missing, never complete.
- **Copy fixes.** Focus questions are labelled «Latihan terbimbing» and keep «Saya belum tahu». S2 and B1 copy no longer promise material the slice does not have.

## How the answers are built

- Every graded question has exactly four options: one key and three distractors. Each distractor records its misconception(s), whether it is serious, its feedback and a short rationale.
- Word meanings reuse the source entry-check option sets (lessons §1.5), so look-alike distractors are source hypotheses, not validated attractors.
- Unit B verb readings cross doer gender with a second taught action; a compound distractor names both misconceptions.
- The validator checks structure only (4 options, 1 key, unique text, metadata, harakat). Tests also check taught-before-use, prior-surface freshness for VRB-01 forms, reservations and lineage. **Pedagogy and keys still need qualified human review.**

## How the app keeps timed checks honest (runtime, this repair)

- **Old exposure counts.** Forms a learner saw under an old ID are reconstructed from the pack version that showed them (never from revised v0.3 wording). Example: an old B2-W4 answer (رَجَعَتْ) makes B3-V3 (رَجَعَتْ) not fresh.
- **Dated teaching views.** Lesson screens passed in a session and screens opened in the Guide are recorded as dated views (append-only). The Guide records a view on every visit, and shows the screen only after that view is saved.
- **Same-day exposure.** A D7/D30 check is not planned on a day its teaching was viewed. If the teaching is viewed after the check was planned (e.g. the learner opens the Guide mid-session), the answer is kept, never re-presented, and marked *not counted* as retention evidence; progress and the session summary say so.
- **Newly introduced teaching (migration).** When a vocabulary table is first shown after the unit's practice (e.g. DEMO-S4 reaching a v0.2 learner), checks that rely on it wait their full interval from that introduction (D7 seven days, D30 thirty days). The original practice anchor and history are unchanged; one-day knowledge is never reported as 7-day retention.
- **Deferred check outcomes.** During a check the app shows only «Jawaban tersimpan» (no key, no right/wrong colour). A check's outcomes stay hidden everywhere (Kemajuan, session summary) until every item of that check (unit × D7/D30) is answered, also when a session ends mid-check or the check continues on a later day. Follow-ups for a check error wait until the check is complete.
- **No mid-check lookup.** While a due check that relies on a word is unanswered, Kemajuan's word list hides that word's meaning.

## Known limits (not resolved by this pack)

- **PED-01 (A3-P03, A3-R01, A3-R02, A3-D7S3):** These four glossed grammar items retain sole ordinary-description answers with matching gender and explicit possession/speech alternatives. Appropriate bounded structural practice; do not report as unaided lexical or broad sentence comprehension.
- **PED-02 (A3-D7S2, A3-D7W5, A3-D30S2, A3-D30W6):** Even deferred feedback leaves shared-form responses within a check block correlated and primed. Avoid interpreting them as independent samples or aggregating duplicate lemma evidence without provenance.
- **PED-03:** Three distinct distractor strings and misconception metadata do not establish three empirically plausible wrong choices. Compound two-error alternatives, source diagnostic look-alikes and unnatural possession phrases need cohort review.
- **PED-04:** Form grids combine pronoun/ending recognition; guided FOCUS combines targeted error with lexical choice; none isolates multiple dimensions or proves production. Practice-only VN distinguishes visible ta endings as intended.
- **PED-05:** No Unit B READY or D7 content remains; single D30 form is insufficient endpoint coverage. Entry/audio diagnostics, transfer, mini readings, verb+doer and speech remain absent/locked. This slice cannot answer the original full trial purpose.
- **Evidence type.** Four-choice recognition cannot establish recall, production, learning gains, transfer or efficacy.

- **Rendering a screen is not learning.** A recorded view shows that teaching was displayed, not that it was read, understood or remembered.
