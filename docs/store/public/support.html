English below

Hubungi kami: support@petalytix.id
Di Android, sebutkan ID pelajar Anda (ada di Pengaturan) agar kami bisa membantu lebih cepat. Jangan mengirim kata sandi atau kode masuk.

Pertanyaan umum

Apakah saya perlu akun?

Tidak. Anda bisa belajar tanpa akun; semua data tersimpan di perangkat. Akun berguna bila Anda ingin kemajuan tersimpan di server dan bisa dipulihkan.

Kode konfirmasi email tidak masuk

Tunggu beberapa menit dan periksa folder Spam atau Promosi. Pastikan alamat email diketik dengan benar, lalu minta kode baru.

Saya lupa kata sandi

Kirim email ke kami dari alamat yang terdaftar, dan kami akan membantu memulihkan akses. Jangan sertakan kata sandi lama.

Jawaban lama saya tidak muncul di akun

Hanya jawaban setelah sinkronisasi dinyalakan yang dikirim. Jawaban sebelumnya tetap ada di perangkat.

Mengapa profil anak tidak bisa disinkronkan?

Sinkronisasi saat ini hanya untuk profil dewasa. Profil anak tetap tersimpan di perangkat.

Pengingat tidak muncul

Pengingat harian ada di Android. Pastikan pengingat menyala di Pengaturan, dan notifikasi untuk AyaIlmu diizinkan di pengaturan HP.

Bagaimana menghapus data saya?

Lihat Hapus akun dan data.

AyaIlmu support

Contact: support@petalytix.id
On Android, include your learner ID (in Settings). Never send your password or sign-in codes.

Do I need an account?

No. You can learn without one and your data stays on the device. An account keeps your progress on our server so it can be restored.

The confirmation email did not arrive

Wait a few minutes and check Spam or Promotions. Check the address you typed, then request a new code.

I forgot my password

Email us from your registered address and we will help you regain access. Do not include your old password.

My earlier answers are not in my account

Only answers given after sync is turned on are sent. Earlier answers stay on the device.

Why can't child profiles sync?

Sync is for adult profiles only for now. Child profiles stay on the device.

The reminder does not appear

Daily reminders are on Android. Check that the reminder is on in Settings and that notifications for AyaIlmu are allowed in your phone's settings.

How do I delete my data?

See Delete account and data.
