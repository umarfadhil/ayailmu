Hapus akun dan data AyaIlmu

AyaIlmu · PT Petalytix Teknologi Indonesia · English below

Di aplikasi (paling cepat)

  1. Buka AyaIlmu. Di Android: Pengaturan → Akun. Di iPhone/iPad: Akun & sinkronisasi di layar utama.
  2. Pilih Hapus akun, lalu Hapus akun permanen (Android) atau Hapus akun saya (iPhone/iPad). Bila diminta, masuk lagi dengan akun yang sama.
  3. Biarkan aplikasi terpasang sampai statusnya selesai. Anda bisa memeriksa status penghapusan tanpa masuk.

Anda juga bisa menghapus data server satu profil saja, tanpa menghapus akun, dari layar akun yang sama (di iPhone/iPad: Hapus data profil di server).

Tanpa aplikasi

Kirim email ke support@petalytix.id dengan judul "Hapus akun AyaIlmu" dari alamat email yang terdaftar. Kami akan membalas untuk memastikan permintaan berasal dari Anda, lalu menghapus akun paling lambat 30 hari setelah konfirmasi. Jangan mengirim kata sandi atau kode masuk.

Apa yang dihapus

  • akun masuk (email dan kata sandi);
  • semua profil di server beserta jawaban, waktu menjawab, nilai, dan kemajuannya;
  • pernyataan dewasa dan catatan persetujuan sinkronisasi.

Apa yang disimpan

  • catatan minimal bahwa akun telah dihapus, tanpa isi belajar, agar data lama tidak terkirim ulang;
  • salinan cadangan basis data, yang dihapus dalam waktu sekitar 3 bulan.

Data di perangkat tidak ikut terhapus. Di Android, hapus lewat Pengaturan → Hapus semua data; di Android maupun iPhone/iPad, Anda juga bisa menghapus aplikasi.

Delete your AyaIlmu account and data

In the app (fastest)

Open AyaIlmu. On Android: Settings → Akun, choose Hapus akun, then Hapus akun permanen. On iPhone/iPad: Account & sync on the main screen, choose Delete entire account, then Delete my account. Sign in again with the same account if asked. Keep the app installed until the status shows it is done; you can check the deletion status without signing in. You can also remove one profile's server data without deleting the account, from the same account screen (on iPhone/iPad: Delete server copy).

Without the app

Email support@petalytix.id with the subject "Delete AyaIlmu account" from your registered address. We will reply to confirm the request came from you and delete the account within 30 days of confirmation. Never send your password or sign-in codes.

What is deleted

Your sign-in account (email and password); every profile on the server with its answers, answer times, scores and progress; your adult declaration and sync consent records.

What is kept

A minimal record that the account was deleted, with no learning content, so old data cannot be re-sent; and database backups, which are removed within about 3 months. Device data is not deleted with the account: on Android use Settings → Hapus semua data; on Android or iPhone/iPad you can also uninstall the app.
