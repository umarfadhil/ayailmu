What AyaIlmu uses
The stroke data in strokes/index.json for the 46 hiragana (keys hira_*) and the 25 Chinese characters
(keys hanzi_*) is derived from KanjiVG: the stroke paths of the KanjiVG files kanji/<code point>.svg
(commit 70a0b7ae0c18ceb5cb358274b029cce0234a43bc) were sampled, scaled from the 109 x 109 canvas to 0..1
and simplified to polylines. Stroke order and direction are KanjiVG's; no strokes were reordered.

These derived entries (the hira_* and hanzi_* entries of strokes/index.json) are shared under the same
licence, CC BY-SA 3.0. You may copy, redistribute and adapt them, including commercially, provided you give
appropriate credit to KanjiVG / Ulrich Apel, link to the licence, indicate if changes were made, and
distribute your contributions under the same licence.

The hangul entries (keys hangul_*) are not from KanjiVG; they were drawn for AyaIlmu by PT Petalytix
Teknologi Indonesia.
